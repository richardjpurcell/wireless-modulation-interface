# WMI-CV2 r3.2c — provisional output protection

Baseline: f964221 (r3.2b SAFE revision).

R21/R27 become 1 kOhm, 0.5 W, 1% candidates. D4/D5 are 1N5817-E3 Schottky candidates, anodes to GND, cathodes to SWOUT_A/B respectively. Logical A/K terminals are not footprint pad numbers. No existing terminal assignment or shared interface changes. Expected: 68 components, 222 terminals, 19 shared nets.

References: https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ADG4612_4613.pdf ; https://www.vishay.com/docs/88525/1n5817.pdf

## Holds

- The clamp does not guarantee safe amplifier or switch current under external drive, including simultaneous faults. +/-12 V is a proposed evaluation envelope, not a rating.
- Verify exact diode ordering suffix, forward voltage and leakage over temperature, switch absolute maximum ratings and powered/unpowered behavior.
- 1 kOhm resistor reduces current but increases load-dependent attenuation. Verify actual Hydrasynth input impedance and calibrate only after standalone qualification.
- Verify startup, shutdown, mute, watchdog transients and ESD protection. Use current-limited bench sources and dummy loads, not musical equipment.
- Native KiCad ERC, integrated netlist verification and PDF inspection pending. Not approved for construction or external CV connection.
