# WMI-PWR revision 2.6a — review only

Native schematic draft; not construction release. Module pin numbers are logical identifiers, not physical header positions. Use terminal markings. U1 BAT- and LOAD- are common ground. USB input is part of U1, not a separate wired terminal. U2 ENABLE is intentionally unconnected. F1 and SW1 ratings are unresolved. C1/C2 are provisional. The library uses project-specific symbols including conventional-looking passive symbols; not official KiCad library symbols. No footprints assigned.

5 V and ground remain local until integration. Expect ERC findings including undriven power inputs; do not add power flags solely to suppress errors. Check expected CSV against exported netlist. Battery and regulator are provisional: at 3.3 V and 80% efficiency, 500 mA at 5 V draws ~0.95 A. Initial battery operation should target <=0.5 A measured input until cell limits verified. Test first with current-limited bench supply, no instrument or battery. Fuse must be coordinated with inrush and wiring; load fuse does not protect every battery-side fault. Verify charger jumper configuration, regulator tolerance, USB separation, shutdown and ripple before release.
