## Budget

5 V average planning allowance 180 mA; peak planning allowance 500 mA. At 3.0 V converter input and 80% efficiency, peak input estimate is 1.04 A, excluding inrush and upstream voltage drop. At 3.7 V nominal, 2 Ah, 85% usable energy and 85% efficiency, runtime at 180 mA is about 5.9 h. These are not measurements.

## Open requirements

- Define normal low-battery cutoff above the cell's 3.0 V end-of-discharge limit, accounting for tolerance, sag and recovery. Boost enable alone is not load isolation. Do not rely on emergency pack protection as normal cutoff.
- Review battery-side wiring protection: existing F1 protects downstream load only, not battery-to-charger wiring. Verify pack PCM and connector ratings; determine whether separate battery-side fuse is warranted.
- Confirm charger battery-to-load current capability and thermal performance, including module connectors. Verify delivered battery and polarity.
- Confirm 5 V and 3.3 V rail margins under processor radio peaks. The Feather regulator rating is shared with its own processor.
- Verify charging settings, temperature monitoring arrangement and charging with load off. No unattended charging.
- Select fuse based on inrush, time-current curve, DC breaking capacity and actual fault current; a fuse is not a precision current limiter.
- Initial bench tests use a current-limited laboratory supply, no battery or synthesizer connected. Test load steps, ripple, startup, shutdown, undervoltage and temperature before battery operation. Use suitable instrumentation, including an oscilloscope for transients.
