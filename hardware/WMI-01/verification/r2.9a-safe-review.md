# WMI-SAFE r2.9a — provisional engineering draft

Do not construct or connect to a synthesizer yet. Local nets only; J3 is a verification placeholder, not a physical connector. All physical pins and ratings require independent review. The A revision of TPS3823 addresses the documented WDI/reset latch behavior. U6 powered by SYS_5V; its open-drain output pulled up to CORE_3V3 drives U5 MR. U5 push-pull output drives U8 open-drain buffer; U8 pulls RESET_N low; R10 pulls it high. U7 qualifies RESET_N and CORE_READY. 1k WDI pulldown per TI. C5 filters SENSE; CT open selects nominal 20ms. U6 MR tied high. R6/R7 give nominal 4.4955 V falling threshold; 0.1% resistors. Verify actual recovery threshold, analog headroom and rail tolerance. U7 output is pre-mute qualification, not proof that relay is energized. SW3 directly opens coil power in mute; second pole grounds status in RUN. D3 cathode at COIL_PLUS, anode at COIL_MINUS. K1 deenergized selects NC ground resistors; permanent jack pulldowns and external fault limiting in CV2. Diode extends relay release time; measure. No hot removal. Firmware must set READY low at reset, initialize DAC, generate heartbeat only after successful processing, and verify timeout and recovery. Watchdog reset pulse is not a latched fault; verify READY drops on reset and does not reassert prematurely. Gate behavior at low or collapsing supply, relay chatter, and reset interface compatibility are unresolved. GPIO and module physical mappings require verification. No claims of fail-safe or certified protection. Physical relay/switch/diode pin mapping not assigned; no footprints.

References: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3823.pdf (sections 5, 6, 7.3.4); https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf (sections 5, 6, 7.3); https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G08 ; https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G07 ; https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-esp32-s3-feather/downloads .
