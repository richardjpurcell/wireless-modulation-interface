# WMI-CORE r2.7b — removable processor design draft

Use 16- and 12-position female sockets on a carrier and matching male headers on the Feather #5323. No separate harness, isolation switch or expansion connector. The U3 module represents the mated header interface; no additional series connector is drawn. J1 remains a temporary logical boundary, included in board netlist but not BOM; remove before PCB work. No physical footprint assigned.

The top-view header position convention L1/R1 starts at the USB end of each row. It is not the manufacturer's pad numbering. 3V2 is the second internally common 3.3 V pad, externally unused; DB is debug UART TX, externally unused. NC in the register means no external carrier connection, not isolation inside the Feather. Existing 52 terminal assignments remain unchanged; two NC terminals are added.

Service: mute, switch off, disconnect charger and CV connections, verify supply rails discharged, remove Feather completely including any auxiliary connections; only then attach USB. Disconnect USB before reinstalling. Never hot-swap. Leave Feather battery and STEMMA QT sockets empty. DAC remains on carrier. Mechanical orientation, header spacing, insertion retention, power-pin loading, footprint and USB backfeed bench qualification outstanding. No construction release.

Firmware: configure I2C_POWER GPIO7 high when needed for onboard pullups; initialize DAC outputs to zero before READY; D13 also drives onboard LED. Reserve unused pins, no expansion hardware yet.
