# WMI-CV2 r3.0a — engineering draft

21 physical components; J6 is a non-BOM temporary boundary. 71 expected terminal assignments.
MCP6002 DIP-8 and ADG4612 TSSOP-16 numerical pin mappings; TS jack identifiers are logical.
ADG4612 IN1/2 isolate DACs, IN3/4 isolate relay outputs; all use OUTPUT_ENABLE. VSS and GND tied to common ground.
The 100k input bias and 1k series resistor attenuate nominal gain slightly. 4 V is a firmware target, not a clamp.
330R 0.5W provisional; negative external faults pass through enabled switches. Do not connect to Hydrasynth until protection is qualified.
Local flags and J6 are temporary and must be removed at integration.
Verify switch logic thresholds, rail sequencing, amplifier headroom, input load, shorts, reverse drive, relay transitions, reset and power-off behavior.
